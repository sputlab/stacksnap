    📖 StackSnap by Sputlab.com

    Goal: stack as many blocks as possible, centering the block that slides back and forth each time. The higher you climb, the faster the game gets and the scene turns to night: how high can you get?

    🎮 Controls: tap the screen, click with the mouse, or press the space bar to drop the block — all three work, pick whichever feels best. Press R at any time to restart instantly.

    Bonuses and power-ups:

    • 🎯 Perfect: land exactly on the block below: 40 points total and extends your streak.
    • 🔥 Streak x2: 3 Perfects in a row activate the x2 multiplier for the next 3 blocks.
    • ⭐ Golden Tower: every 5 blocks, the next one is worth +50 extra points.
    • 💎 Bonus crystals: appear now and then and last for the next 3 blocks:
      • 🐢 Blue (Slowmo): slows down the sliding block.
      • 🎯 Orange (Precision): a gap of up to 10% from the block below still counts as Perfect.
      • 💥 Red (Double Points): every block is worth double the points.
    • 💚 Perfect Zone: every 10 blocks a green line appears: hit it for triple points!
    • 🛟 Safety net: the first time the block gets too narrow to continue, it's automatically restored — this happens only once per game.
    • 🎉 Milestones: every 25 blocks triggers a little celebration with confetti and a fanfare.

    Unlock achievements and climb the local leaderboard to challenge your records — check out the other menu items too.

    🌍 Global Leaderboard: at the end of a run you can switch from the Local leaderboard (only on your device) to the Global one (shared with every player) using the switch at the top. Saving your name also submits your score to the global leaderboard, which is permanent and has no limits — tied scores share the same rank.
